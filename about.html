About Us

About Aecons

  • Established in 2019 under the leadership of Daniel Talion, Aecons is a multidisciplinary Architecture and Engineering Design Consultancy based in Quezon City.
  • We specialize in design and build solutions that integrate creativity, technical precision, and sustainability.
  • Our expertise spans residential, commercial, and industrial projects across the Philippines.
  • Aecons believes that architecture is not just about aesthetics — it’s about design that enhances everyday life.
  • Each project begins with collaboration, grounded in a clear understanding of client goals, site context, and community impact.

Our Commitment

  • Aecons is dedicated to design excellence and constructive innovation in every project we undertake.
  • We uphold a client-centered approach, ensuring each space reflects the user’s identity and purpose.
  • Our team combines architectural vision, engineering expertise, and construction efficiency for seamless project execution.
  • We are committed to craftsmanship, sustainability, and ethical practice in all aspects of design and construction.
  • Through collaboration, we aim to build lasting relationships with clients, partners, and communities.

Vision

  • To redefine the built environment through creative, sustainable, and human-centered design.
  • To become a leading design and build firm in the Philippines, recognized for innovation, integrity, and impact.
  • To create architecture that inspires, endures, and enriches the lives of those who experience it.

Mission

  • To integrate design creativity and engineering precision in crafting spaces that respond to both form and function.
  • To provide comprehensive, efficient, and sustainable solutions — from concept to completion.
  • To deliver projects that reflect the client’s vision, while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and sustainability.
  • To foster a culture of learning, collaboration, and design excellence within our team.

Goals

  • Deliver timely, high-quality, and sustainable projects across all sectors.
  • Ensure cost-efficient design and build solutions without compromising aesthetics or functionality.
  • Expand Aecons’ reach nationwide while maintaining our personalized service and design integrity.
  • Adopt green and energy-efficient practices as standard in every project.
  • Strengthen partnerships with clients and collaborators built on trust, transparency, and performance.

Core Values – D.E.S.I.G.N.

  • D – Dedication to Craft
    → We commit to excellence and attention to detail in every stage of design and construction.
  • E – Environmental Responsibility
    → We advocate sustainable design practices that respect nature and reduce environmental impact.
  • S – Service and Collaboration
    → We listen, communicate, and work closely with our clients to achieve shared success.
  • I – Innovation in Design and Process
    → We embrace new technologies, creative thinking, and adaptive solutions.
  • G – Growth through Learning
    → We continuously develop our skills and knowledge to improve design outcomes.
  • N – Nurturing Communities
    → We design with empathy, ensuring our work positively contributes to the people and places we serve.
